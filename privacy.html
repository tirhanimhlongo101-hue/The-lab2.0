What we don't do

  • We don't run advertising trackers, ad pixels, or sell your data to advertisers.
  • We don't currently run analytics software (no Google Analytics or similar) — this may change, and if it does, this policy will be updated first.
  • We don't access your social media accounts. Nothing here connects to Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube on your behalf.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run The Lab. Each only receives what it needs to do its job:

ProviderWhat it handles
SupabaseYour account, authentication, and all database records described above
VercelHosting the website itself; sees standard server logs
Anthropic (Claude)Processes the text you submit to the AI website builder/editor (your bio, niche, site content, and edit instructions) in order to generate a response. This text is sent from our server, not your browser.
Google FontsLoads typefaces used on this site. Your browser connects directly to Google's servers to fetch font files, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request, separately from us.

We do not sell personal data to any third party.

Public vs. private data — read this carefully

Some of what you enter into The Lab is intentionally public — your display name, bio, published site content, and any deal you mark "public" are visible to anyone who visits your page, with or without an account. Some is always private — your email address, unpublished draft edits, and any deal you mark "private" are never shown to anyone but you.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account [self-service deletion is not yet built — contact us directly to request deletion in the meantime], we will delete your profile and deal records from our active database. Some data may persist briefly in backups before being purged.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. If you're in South Africa, this is governed by POPIA; if you're in the EU/UK, by GDPR; other regions have their own equivalent laws. To exercise any of these rights right now, contact us directly — self-service tools for this are on the roadmap but not built yet.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you logged in — this is strictly necessary for the site to function and isn't used for tracking or advertising. See our cookie notice for details.

Children

The Lab is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.
