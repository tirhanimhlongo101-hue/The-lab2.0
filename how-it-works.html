How it works

From messy DMs to one trusted link.

Here's exactly what happens when you join The Lab, from your first deal to your public portfolio.

1

Claim your link

Pick your thelab.me/yourname link — this becomes your public page and the one link you put in every bio, comment, and media kit from now on.

2

Log your brand deals

Add each brand collab as it happens — payment, deliverables, usage rights, dates. It takes a couple of minutes and builds your deal history automatically.

3

Get warned before you sign anything bad

Paste in a brand's terms before you agree to anything. The Lab checks for the patterns that burn creators — missing payment amounts, unlimited usage rights, no exclusivity cap — and flags them in plain language.

4

Your portfolio builds itself

Every completed deal you choose to make public shows up on your public page automatically — brands you've worked with, campaigns you're proud of, no manual updating.

5

Send a media kit in one click

Your audience numbers, top brand partners, and past collabs are pulled straight from your profile into a ready-to-send media kit, instead of rebuilding a slide deck every time a brand asks.

6

Add your own products & affiliate links

List your own products or software, or drop affiliate links to brands on your deal history — shown right on your page, linking straight out to wherever people check out. No listing fees.

7

Browse brand opportunities Early access

Your dashboard has a curated feed of open brand calls you can browse and mark interest in. It's early — not yet personalized to your niche, and there's no outreach automation behind it — but it's live today, not just a roadmap idea.
