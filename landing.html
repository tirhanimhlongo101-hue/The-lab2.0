Questions? Answered

How The Lab works.

Is The Lab free to use?⌄

Yes — the Free plan covers your public portfolio page (Editorial or Paper theme), manual deal logging for up to 5 deals, and your Shop section. No card required. Every other theme, custom colors, the deal red-flag checker, brand opportunities feed, and analytics unlock on Pro.

How does bad-deal flagging work?⌄

You paste in the deal terms and The Lab checks for common red flags — missing payment amounts, unlimited usage rights, vague deliverables, and more.

Do brands see my deal history?⌄

You control it per deal. Each deal you log has a public/private toggle in your dashboard — public deals show on your page (brand name, platform, payout amount); private ones stay visible only to you. Your full contract terms and notes are never shown publicly either way, just the summary.

Will you find brand deals for me?⌄

A basic version is live — your dashboard has a curated feed of open brand opportunities you can browse and mark interest in. It's not personalized to your niche yet and there's no outreach automation behind it — that's still on the roadmap. Today, The Lab is mainly for tracking and vetting the deals you already have coming in.

Do I need a big following to join?⌄

No — The Lab is built for creators at any size, including micro-creators who are often the most underserved by brand deals.
